Help & rating rules

How match logging, peer confirmation, and club ratings work in RallyRate.

Match logging & verification

Every match recorded in RallyRate starts in Pending status until an opposing player confirms the final score.

  1. 01
    Scoring format

    Games are played to at least 11 points and must be won by at least 2 points (single game or best of 3).

  2. 02
    Peer confirmation

    Only an opposing player (or a club organizer) can confirm a submitted result. Submitters and partners cannot self-verify.

  3. 03
    Dispute handling

    If a score or roster has an error, an opponent can report an issue to hold the match for club organizer review.

ELO RATING ENGINE
