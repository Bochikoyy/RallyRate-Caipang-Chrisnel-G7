Help & rating rules

How RallyRate calculates 1.000–8.000 DUPR-style performance ratings across club events and peer matches.

1.000 – 8.000 performance formula

Every player starts at 3.000 with separate Doubles and Singles ratings. Ratings update immediately when a club host records an event score or an opponent confirms a peer match.

  1. 01
    Margin of victory & expectations

    Team ratings are averaged in doubles. Your rating shift compares your actual win + point share against your expected score share — winning 11–2 earns a much larger gain than 11–9.

  2. 02
    0.500 mismatch protection

    When opposing teams differ by 0.500 or more and the favored side wins, ratings stay unchanged (0.000 delta) so favorites are never penalized for close recreational games. Upsets still reward underdogs in full.

  3. 03
    Match source & reliability weighting

    Host-verified club session games carry full 1.0x weight, while peer-confirmed casual matches carry 0.6x weight. Newer players calibrate faster with higher K-factors until reliability climbs toward 100%.
